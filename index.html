How are you different from other AI consultants?

Most AI consultants just give you advice. They write a report and leave. We're different because we actually build the thing. No. You can stop after any step. Most people start small with our 1-week Audit or 2-week Assessment to see if we're a good fit. If you like the results, we keep going. If not, you walk away with useful answers and owe us nothing more. We earn your trust before we ask for a bigger commitment.
