This Cookie Policy explains how netaesthetics.ai uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function, work more efficiently, and provide reporting information. "First-party cookies" are set by the website you're visiting. "Third-party cookies" are set by other services used on the site.

3. Your Cookie Choices

When you first visit our website, a cookie consent banner gives you the choice to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site. You can also control cookies through your browser settings:

  • View and delete stored cookies individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies from being set
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

4. International Data Transfers

Some cookie providers may transfer data outside the EEA/UK. Appropriate safeguards are in place, including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. See our Privacy Policy for details.
